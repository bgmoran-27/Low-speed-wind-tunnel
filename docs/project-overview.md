# Project Overview

## Project

Instrumented Low-Speed Wind Tunnel

## Start Date

August 10, 2026

## Objective

Build and characterize a small low-speed wind tunnel and use it to experimentally investigate aerodynamic behavior of a 3D-printed airfoil.

## Initial Research Question

How does increasing the angle of attack of a small airfoil affect its measured lift and observed airflow behavior?

## What I Plan to Measure

1. Airspeed inside the test section
2. Airflow uniformity across the test section
3. Airfoil angle of attack
4. Lift force produced by the airfoil
5. Visible airflow behavior using tufts

## Initial Project Stages

1. Study existing wind-tunnel designs
2. Select overall tunnel architecture
3. Design custom test section
4. Build mechanical tunnel
5. Measure and improve airflow quality
6. Build airfoil mounting system
7. Add airspeed measurement
8. Add lift measurement
9. Conduct aerodynamic experiments
10. Analyze results
11. Document failures and redesigns
12. Produce final technical report and portfolio
